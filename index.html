Field needs an odd Shape Size (3, 5, 7, 9).
A free endpoint cannot be placed outside the net (Single and Field are closed nets).
Curve, free-clothing and face-fill modes are off while a net transform is active: faces are only exact on a Field warp, and lines are kept straight. Set the net back to Regular (Base sheet, square) to use them again.
The cross-layer overlay is on and covers the sheet colors (Compute Cross-Layer Faces to refresh, or edit a sheet to clear it).
This layer is a timeline keyframe: playback crossfades its colors with the neighboring keyframe's (in linear light).

How to use:

  • Click nodes to connect them.
  • Use the controls to change shape, size and node count.
  • Toggle visibility of nodes and curvature.
